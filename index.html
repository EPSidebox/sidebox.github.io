Greenlist~→

A real AI text watermark, run without the restraint it depends on. A secret key decides which words are permitted at each position; forbidden ones are overwritten with permitted ones, and the certification score climbs as the passage comes apart. Three vocabulary sources, word and field views, fully offline.
